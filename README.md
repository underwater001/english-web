# English Web

**Practise SSC CGL English the way the exam asks it.** Every question tells you *why* the right option is right, and *why each wrong option fails*.

Live site: https://underwater001.github.io/english-web/

## What you can do

- **Learn a topic.** Open a topic such as *Subject-verb agreement*. The rule comes first, with one right and one wrong example. Then each question type: how to spot it, the fast way, the traps.
- **Practise.** Questions run easiest first. Pick an option and you see the reason, the fastest way in, and a note on every wrong option, with the common mistakes marked.
- **Read daily.** Each editorial becomes a short capsule of words and ideas, a passage, and a quiz that tests every word.
- **Build vocabulary.** The word bank holds every word, idiom, one-word substitution and phrasal verb, with synonyms, antonyms and an example. Tick what you know.
- **Mix it up.** Mixed practice pulls questions from every topic. Filter by format, level, or only the ones you got wrong.

Your progress is saved in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Feeding it

Send me any of these and it is filed, checked and published:

| You send | You get |
|---|---|
| An SSC CGL English paper (PDF or photos) | Every question playable, sorted by topic and format, with the paper's link on the Sources page |
| An editorial or newspaper page | A daily drop: capsule, passage in our own words, quiz |
| A rule sheet, word list or idiom list | Rules on the topic page and entries in the word bank |

Nothing goes live unless every check passes.

## Can you trust the answers?

Checked by the build, every time:
- every wrong option has its own specific note,
- synonym, antonym, idiom and one-word answers are matched against the stored word facts,
- a past question must agree with its paper file on the key and the answer text,
- every daily word is in the capsule *and* tested.

**Not checked by a machine:** whether a grammar rule is applied correctly in a sentence. Those answers rest on the rule shown on the topic page. Where we worked out a past paper's key ourselves instead of copying an official one, the question says so.

## Rules of the project

- Nothing unverified goes in.
- No PDFs and no copied passages in this public repo. Papers and articles are stored as short restatements in our own words, with paper and question number.
- Paper and source links show only on the Sources page.
